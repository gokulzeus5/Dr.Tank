# Dr. Tank – Professional Water Tank Cleaning Website

> **Tagline:** Your Water's Best Friend  
> **Location:** Palakkad & All 14 Districts of Kerala, India  
> **Contact / WhatsApp:** +91 80897 83517  
> **Email:** dr.tankpalakkad@gmail.com  

A modern, high-performance, single-page website for **Dr. Tank**, Kerala's premier professional water tank cleaning service provider. The site is optimized to convert visitors into qualified service enquiries via direct WhatsApp integration and phone connectivity.

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)
- **UI Library:** React.js & TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom brand palette
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Optimization:** Next.js Image component (WebP / AVIF responsive formats)
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5, Schema.org LocalBusiness, ItemList/Service, and FAQPage JSON-LD, automated XML sitemap (`sitemap.xml`) and `robots.txt`

## 📑 Website Structure (Exact Order)

1. **Section 01 – Sticky Navigation Bar**
   - Left: Dr. Tank brand mark + tagline *"Your Water's Best Friend"*.
   - Right: Home, About, Services, Why Choose Us, Contact + *"Book a Cleaning"* direct CTA.
   - Mobile: Responsive hamburger drawer with touch-friendly booking buttons.

2. **Section 02 – Hero Section**
   - Headline: *"Clean Water Starts With a Clean Tank."*
   - Description emphasizing residential, commercial, and industrial tank cleaning.
   - Dual CTAs: *"Book Your Cleaning"* and *"WhatsApp Us"*.
   - Trust indicators for Residential & Commercial, Industrial, and Kerala-Wide service.
   - Primary SEO phrase naturally integrated: *"Water Tank Cleaning Services in Kerala"*.

3. **Section 03 – Introduction / About Dr. Tank**
   - Core philosophy: *"Your Water's Best Friend"*.
   - 4 service focus areas: Residential, Commercial, Industrial, and Treatment Facilities.
   - Direct *"Talk to Our Team"* WhatsApp CTA.

4. **Section 04 – Complete Water Tank Cleaning Solutions**
   - **Service 01:** Residential Water Tank Cleaning (overhead sintex & underground sumps).
   - **Service 02:** Commercial Water Tank Cleaning (apartments, hotels, offices).
   - **Service 03:** STP Tank Cleaning (Sewage Treatment Plants).
   - **Service 04:** ETP Tank Cleaning (Effluent Treatment Plants).
   - **Service 05:** WTP & Industrial Processing Tank Cleaning.
   - Each card features custom imagery, key process points, contextual SEO tags, and an *"Enquire on WhatsApp"* CTA with pre-filled text.

5. **Section 05 – Why Choose Dr. Tank?**
   - Specialized Cleaning Solutions
   - Residential to Industrial Expertise
   - Kerala-Wide Service Coverage
   - Professional Approach
   - Easy WhatsApp Booking

6. **Section 06 – Our Work / Gallery**
   - High-resolution visual showcase with category filter pills (Overhead, Underground, Commercial, Industrial, STP/ETP, Equipment).
   - Interactive zoom & inspection modal lightbox.

7. **Section 07 – Service Packages**
   - **BASIC:** Regular maintenance, sludge removal & mechanical scrub (*"Contact for Pricing"*).
   - **ADVANCED:** Deep mechanical rotary jet wash & antibacterial treatment (*"Get a Custom Quote"*).
   - **AMC:** Annual Maintenance Contract for periodic upkeep (*"Get a Custom Quote"*).

8. **Section 08 – Service Locations Across Kerala**
   - Clean, responsive grid listing all **14 Kerala districts**:
     *Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Kottayam, Idukki, Ernakulam, Thrissur, Palakkad, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur, Kasaragod*.
   - Direct CTA: *"Check Service Availability"* on WhatsApp.

9. **Section 09 – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**
   - Interactive accordion addressing all 9 client concerns (methods, pricing factors, AMC, coverage, cleaning machines/liquids clarification).

10. **Section 10 – Book Your Service (Lead Generation Form)**
    - Validates Full Name, Indian 10-digit mobile number, preferred service date, service type, and Kerala district.
    - Encodes and opens a structured booking enquiry on WhatsApp (`+91 80897 83517`).
    - Clear booking disclaimer noting team availability confirmation.

11. **Section 11 – Contact Section**
    - Click-to-call (`tel:+918089783517`), WhatsApp Chat, and direct email (`dr.tankpalakkad@gmail.com`).
    - Service coverage & operational hours.

12. **Section 12 – Footer**
    - Brand tagline, quick links, service category index, contact info, and copyright statement.

13. **Mobile Bottom Sticky CTA Bar & Floating WhatsApp Button**
    - Fixed bottom dual-action bar (*"Call Now"* + *"WhatsApp"*) with minimum 44px touch targets.
    - Floating pinging WhatsApp shortcut button.

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Run the development server
npm run dev

# Or build and run production server
npm run build
npm run start
```

Visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
